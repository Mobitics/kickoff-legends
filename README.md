# Kickoff Legends

A 3D soccer game that runs in the browser. Play it at https://mobitics.github.io/kickoff-legends/

- **Play Now**: 1 vs 1, 2 vs 2, 11 vs 11 or penalties. Pick your team and your opponent from 43 real clubs.
- **My Team**: edit your squad with player cards and pick home and away kits.
- **My Player**: start in a small town and get scouted to bigger clubs.
- **Manager Mode**: pick a real club, trade players, and watch or simulate a full season.

Controls: on a phone, use the joystick and buttons. On a keyboard, move with arrows or WASD, shoot with Space, pass with X, steal or stepover with C, body or spin with V, flick with F, and sprint with Shift.

Player ratings, stats and squads are estimates for a fan game. Progress is saved in your browser.
